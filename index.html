The Summit Tasting is a 90-minute experience for people who want to understand what is inside their cup. We pour three coffees side by side, teach you to smell, sip, and describe them, and show you how the brewing method changes the flavor.

Why climb with us

  • Guided by trained baristas

    Every session is led by a barista who answers your questions and helps you find the right words for what you taste.

  • Three coffees, one table

    You compare origins and brewing methods side by side, so the differences become easy to notice, even if this is your first tasting.

  • Take the mountain home

    Every guest leaves with 250 grams of the featured coffee and a postcard-size brewing guide with recipes for French press, moka pot, Chemex, and V60.

This season’s summit: Colibrí

Colibrí grows at Finca El Paraíso, in the Ojo de Agua area of Riosucio, Caldas, in the heart of Colombia’s coffee region. At about 1,800 meters above sea level, the mountain air is cool and the cherries ripen slowly, and slow ripening gives the coffee more sugar and a cleaner taste.

How the flavor climbs

  1. PanelaThe first sip is sweet and round, like warm brown sugar.
  2. Red appleIn the middle, the cup turns juicy and fresh.
  3. Orange peelThe finish is clean, citrusy, and long.

Two ways to pour it

V60

A cone-shaped dripper with a paper filter. It makes a clean, bright cup that highlights the fruit in the coffee.

Chemex

A glass brewer with a thick filter. It removes the oils and creates a smooth, elegant cup with a soft sweetness.

Our starting recipe uses a ratio of one to fifteen: 20 grams of ground coffee for 300 milliliters of water at 92 degrees Celsius.
